A. What Are Verbs?

Verbs are important words that tell us what a person, animal, or thing does. They help us understand the actions happening around us.

We use verbs every day when we talk about our activities, describe what we do, and share our experiences.

Let's explore some examples!

The girl reads a storybook.
The dog runs across the yard.
The children dance together.
The mother cooks dinner.
The boy kicks the ball.

The highlighted words are verbs because they tell us the actions performed by the subjects.

B. Learning Objectives

After completing this lesson, learners should be able to:

  • Explain the meaning of verbs in their own words.
  • Recognize action words in pictures and sentences.
  • Identify verbs used in everyday activities.
  • Distinguish verbs from other words in simple sentences.
  • Create simple sentences using appropriate action words.

We can find action words in many situations.

Students listen to the teacher.

Mark opens his book.

During Playtime

The children run around.
The boy throws a ball.
The girls skip together.

E. Why Should We Learn Verbs?

Learning verbs helps us:

  • Describe the activities we do every day.
  • Understand what is happening in a sentence.
  • Express our thoughts clearly.
  • Improve our speaking and writing skills.
  • Create meaningful sentences and stories.

Remember This!

VERBS = ACTION WORDS!

Every time someone walks, sings, reads, or plays, an action is taking place. These actions can be expressed through verbs.

Keep your eyes open! You can discover verbs everywhere.
