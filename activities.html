Mission: Become an Action Explorer!

A. Your Turn to Explore!

Now that you know how to recognize verbs, it's your turn to use them!

In this activity, you will observe your surroundings, identify actions, and write your own sentences. You will become an Action Explorer by discovering verbs in your daily routine.

B. Learning Objectives

After completing the activity, learners should be able to:

  • Recognize action words in their surroundings.
  • Identify verbs through pictures and actual movements.
  • Construct simple sentences using verbs.
  • Share examples of actions performed every day.

C. Your Mission: Become an Action Explorer!

Instructions:

  1. Observe the activities happening around your home or classroom.
  2. Identify five different actions performed by people or animals.
  3. Write the correct verb for each action.
  4. Create a sentence using every verb you identified.
  5. Share your completed work with your teacher or classmates.

Example:

What I ObservedVerb
A person reading a bookRead
A child moving quicklyRun
A mother preparing foodCook
A dog making a soundBark
A girl making a pictureDraw
